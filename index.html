Finn — Lost & Found Concierge

AI chat agent prototype · Retell AI · Google Apps Script · Cal.com

Click the "Lost something?" button (bottom-right) to report a lost item. Finn searches found items, verifies ownership, and books a pickup.

Try this:
1. Say: I lost my black iPhone near gate B12 yesterday, clear case
2. When asked the verification question, answer: golden retriever
3. Pick a pickup slot and enter your name + email.
Other scenarios: a grey North Face backpack at baggage claim · AirPods at the Plane Train · a red guitar (no match)

Demo only. Sample data, not a real airport service.

Finn – AI Lost & Found Airport Concierge

A prototype chat assistant for an airport lost & found desk. Simply describe what you misplaced, and Finn will check the found-items list, confirm the item belongs to you, and help you schedule a convenient pickup time.

Use case

Most lost & found desks rely on phone calls, paper forms and spreadsheets that staff search by hand. This prototype explores how a chat assistant can handle the routine parts of that process, so travelers get answers sooner and staff can focus on the cases that need personal attention.

Travelers

Describe the item in your own words and learn quickly whether it has been turned in.

Staff

Searching, ownership checks and scheduling are handled by the assistant. Staff step in only when a case needs their judgment.

Records

Each conversation is recorded with its outcome, giving the desk a clear history of every request.

How it works

Finn is built as a conversation flow in Retell AI. Each step has a single purpose, and the backend is called at defined points so the results are consistent.

1Greet & intakeName, item, color, where, when
2Normalize"specs" → eyewear, "yesterday" → date
3SearchFuzzy-match unclaimed items
4ConfirmDescribe match with public details only
5VerifyOwner-only question, checked server-side
6BookLive slots from Cal.com
7LogCase ID + claim code

Matching

Each found item gets a score out of 100 based on category, color, location, date and keywords. Common synonyms are handled (e.g. "specs" → eyewear, "rucksack" → bag). Up to 3 items scoring 45 or more are returned.

Verification

Each item has a question only the owner should be able to answer. The answer is checked by the backend, not the AI, and a small difference in wording is accepted. A correct answer generates a claim code.

Booking and logging

Pickup slots come from Cal.com, which also sends the confirmation email. The case is saved in the Cases tab with the outcome, claim code and pickup time.

Post-chat summary

When a chat ends, Retell sends a summary and sentiment through a webhook, and these are added to the same case row.

Architecture

The prototype runs entirely on free tiers. The assistant does not read the spreadsheet directly; it can only call three actions on the Apps Script backend.

Try it: the happy path

Please click "Lost something?" in the bottom-right corner and follow the steps below. The sample data includes 14 items, such as phones, bags, a wallet, AirPods, a passport and sunglasses.

  1. Share any name, for example Alex
  2. Say: I lost my black iPhone near gate B12 yesterday, it has a clear case
  3. Finn describes a match (black phone, Concourse B Gate 12) → reply yes
  4. Verification question: "What is on your lock screen wallpaper?" → reply a golden retriever
  5. Provide an email address you can check, then choose one of the offered time slots
  6. Finn confirms the pickup and gives you a claim code and case ID. Cal.com sends a confirmation email.

Error conditions & edge cases

Finn is designed to respond helpfully when a conversation doesn't follow the expected path. You're welcome to try any of the following.

ScenarioTry sayingWhat Finn does
No match"I lost a red guitar"Apologizes, asks for an email address and opens a case (no_match) so staff can follow up.
Wrong answerAnswer the verification question incorrectlyPolitely lets you know the answer doesn't match and invites you to try again.
Three wrong answersAnswer incorrectly 3 timesThe item is held for review (locked_review) and you're kindly asked to verify in person with a photo ID.
Asking for details"What brand is it? What's on the sticker?"Courteously declines. To protect the owner, the assistant only receives the category, color, location and date.
Vague item"A document"Asks one clarifying question, such as whether it's a passport, license, ID card or boarding pass.
Unknown location"I don't remember where"Asks one helpful follow-up question, then continues the search without a location.
Slot takenPick a slot someone else just bookedOffers another available slot instead.
Declines pickup"I don't want to pick it up now"Offers to hold the item for 30 days for a walk-in pickup, or to close the case (hold_walk_in / declined).
Gives up"Not needed" during verificationThanks you, explains the in-person option and records the case as abandoned.
Urgent"I need my passport, my flight is in an hour"Recommends visiting Guest Services in person right away.

Future enhancements

  • Item photos. Store a photo with each found item and show it after the owner passes verification, so they can confirm it's theirs before coming in.
  • Report a found item. Let travelers or staff tell the agent about something they found. It would collect the details (type, color, brand, where and when it was found, and a verification question) and add a new row to the FoundItems tab.
  • Notify when a match turns up. Email people with open cases when a newly reported item matches their description.

Please note: this is a prototype that uses fictional sample data and is not an actual airport service. Thank you for taking the time to try it.
